## What we build

SupplyzPro is a business management platform available in English and French. One workspace covers:

| Area | What it handles |
| --- | --- |
| **Inventory** | Stock, products and movements |
| **Sales & Purchases** | Quotes, invoices, orders, suppliers |
| **Accounting** | Books and tax calculation for accountants |
| **HR & Projects** | People and work in the same place |
| **POS** | Point of sale connected to stock |
| **Analytics** | Reporting across all of the above |

Access depends on role, subscription and company configuration.
